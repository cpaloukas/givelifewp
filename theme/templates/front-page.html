Why it matters

Blood can't be made. Only given.

Every transfusion starts with someone who took an hour out of their day. Blood doesn't keep for long, so hospitals rely on people giving regularly, not just when there's an emergency on the news.

  • Up to 3 people can be helped by one whole blood donation.
  • About 1 hour for the whole visit, with around 10 minutes of actual giving.
  • About 1 week is all donated platelets last.

Why and how to give blood

Ways to give

  • Whole blood Where almost everyone starts. About 10 minutes in the chair.
  • Platelets Vital for cancer treatment and surgery, and they only last a week.
  • Plasma Used for burns and trauma, and to make life-saving medicines.
