Why it matters

Blood can’t be made. Only given.

Every transfusion starts with someone who took an hour out of their day. Blood doesn’t keep for long, so hospitals rely on people giving regularly, not just when there’s an emergency on the news.

Why and how to give blood

Three ways to give

Start with whole blood. Go further if you can.

  • Whole blood About 10 min · every 8–16 weeks Where almost everyone starts. Goes to surgery, trauma, childbirth and more.
  • Platelets About 90 min · as often as every 1–2 weeks Keep children with leukaemia and people in cancer treatment going. They only last a week.
  • Plasma About 45 min · as often as every 1–2 weeks Used for burns and bleeding, and to make medicines for immune and clotting disorders.
