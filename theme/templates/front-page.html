Coming soon

Sometimes a reminder is all it takes.

GiveLifeWP is just an idea for a non-commercial community project with one hope: that more people give, starting with blood. We're gathering clear answers, honest guidance and the occasional gentle nudge, for whenever you're ready.

  • Give blood.
  • Give hope.
  • Give life!

First up: why blood donation matters, who can give, and what really happens on the day. Later: organ and tissue donation, first aid, CPR, bleeding control and everyday preparedness.

Until then, if you're able, look up your local blood service and book a donation. It's one of the simplest ways to help another person directly.

How it started

With one tweet. Christos nudged people to go give blood, and Ben replied that the nudge worked: signed up. One tweet, one new donor. We'd like a few more of those.

Born out of WordPress® community people, made for everyone. Whether you build websites or have never heard of a plugin, you're welcome here.

Not a business. No ads, nothing for sale. Just people encouraging people.
